1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise contact us.

  • Personal Information: We collect email addresses, usernames, and contact telephone numbers provided during registration.
  • Payment Data: We may collect data necessary to process your in-app purchase payments if you choose to make purchases. All in-app purchase payment data is handled, processed, and securely stored by Google Play Billing. KodIT does not collect or store your payment instrument or credit card numbers.
  • Application Data: We may request permissions to send you push notifications regarding your account or certain features of the application via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). You can opt out in your device settings anytime.

6. DO WE USE COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and store your information.

Google AdMob & Third-Party Advertising Disclosure

We use Google AdMob to display mobile advertisements within the EngageX application. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device-specific data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID): A user-resettable unique identifier for advertising.
  • Device & Interaction Data: Operating system, mobile network, ad impressions, and click events.

This data is used to provide relevant ads, detect invalid traffic/fraud, and measure ad performance.

Rewards from Advertisements Are Non-Withdrawable: Coins earned by watching rewarded advertisements within the App are promotional, virtual utility credits intended solely for use inside EngageX, such as uploading and promoting videos. These coins have no monetary value, are not eligible for cash-out, and cannot be withdrawn, redeemed, or exchanged for cash or any other form of real-world payment. Only coins earned through eligible video-watching activity may be submitted for withdrawal, subject to our Terms and Conditions.

Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based ads anytime in your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete advertising ID).

For more details, review Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.
